# 📊 Olist E-Commerce Business Intelligence Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Power BI dashboard** built using the Olist E-Commerce dataset.
The dashboard provides deep insights into sales performance, customer behavior, seller performance, and overall business growth from 2016 to 2018.

The report is divided into four main sections:

* Executive Overview
* Sales Analysis
* Customer Analysis
* Seller Dashboard.

---

# 1️⃣ Executive Overview

<img src="https://i.postimg.cc/vTSFgrQp/Executive-Overview.png" width="100%">

## 🔎 Key Insights

* 🛍 **33K Total Products** available on the platform
* 💰 **R$ 16.01M Total Revenue** generated
* 📦 **99.44K Total Orders** placed
* 👥 **96.10K Total Customers**
* ⭐ **Average Review Score: 4.09**
* 🚚 **Average Shipping Cost: R$ 19.99**

### 📈 Business Insights

* Revenue shows steady growth from **2016 to 2018**.
* Majority of revenue is generated during mid-year months.
* Customer satisfaction is high (rating above 4).
* Order volume significantly increased in 2017 and 2018.

---

# 2️⃣ Sales Analysis

<img src="https://i.postimg.cc/qvKVq6Wn/Sales-Analysis.png" width="100%">

## 🔎 Key Insights

* 💳 **Credit Card** is the most preferred payment method.
* 🌍 Major revenue is concentrated in **Brazil**, especially in South-East regions.
* 🏆 Top product categories by revenue:

  * beleza_saude
  * informatica_acessorios
  * cama_mesa_banho
* 📦 Some orders contain more than 20 items.
* 📅 Monthly revenue peaks around **May to August**.

### 📊 Business Insights

* Credit card payments dominate the transaction volume.
* Certain product categories contribute significantly to overall revenue.
* Revenue distribution is geographically concentrated.
* Seasonal spikes are visible in monthly sales trends.

---

# 3️⃣ Customer Analysis

<img src="https://i.postimg.cc/prr3HmDB/Customer-Analysis.png" width="100%">

## 🔎 Key Insights

* 📍 Customers are highly concentrated in Brazil’s South-East region.
* 📦 Most orders are successfully **delivered**.
* 📊 Average Order Value per customer is well distributed.
* 👤 A small group of customers contributes to higher order volumes.
* 📈 Customer base increased significantly from 2016 to 2018.

### 📊 Business Insights

* Strong customer growth trend across years.
* High delivery success rate indicates operational efficiency.
* Customer purchasing behavior shows moderate repeat buying patterns.
* Revenue contribution is slightly skewed toward top customers.

# 4️⃣ Seller Dashboard

<img src="https://i.postimg.cc/5tJGt7BY/Seller-Dashboard.png" width="100%">

## 🔎 Key Insights

* 🏪 **3095 Total Sellers**
* 📦 **112.65K Products Sold**
* 💰 **R$ 15.84M Total Revenue**
* 🏆 Top sellers contribute a significant portion of total revenue.
* 📍 Sellers are mainly concentrated in specific Brazilian states.
* 📦 Some sellers handle more than 2K orders.

### 📊 Business Insights

* Revenue is not evenly distributed among sellers.
* A small percentage of sellers generate a large portion of total revenue.
* Seller distribution is geographically concentrated.
* Product diversity varies significantly across sellers.
